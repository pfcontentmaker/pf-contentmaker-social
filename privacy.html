Privacy Policy

PF.Contentmaker Social · Effective 31 July 2026

PF.Contentmaker Social (“the App”) is a private tool operated by Felipe Puerta, an individual developer. It is used solely to manage social media accounts that the operator owns and controls. The App is not offered to the general public and has no user accounts of its own.

1. Who this policy covers

Only the operator’s own accounts. The App does not onboard third parties, does not collect data about other people, and cannot be authorized by any account other than those explicitly registered by the operator.

2. What data the App accesses

When an account owned by the operator authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App may read:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic) — open ID, avatar and display name.
  • Extended profile information (user.info.profile) — profile link, bio description and verification status.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats) — follower count, following count, likes count and video count.
  • Public video list (video.list) — the account’s own public videos and their metrics.

The App does not access private messages, contacts, payment information, location, or any data belonging to other TikTok users.

3. Why the data is accessed

Exclusively to record how the operator’s own accounts perform over time, and to inform the operator’s own content decisions. The data is never used for advertising, profiling of other people, or any automated decision affecting anyone else.

4. How the data is stored

Metrics are written to local files on the operator’s own computer. Access tokens are stored locally in configuration files and are never transmitted to any third party. The App has no public server, no shared database and no analytics service.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, published, or shared with any third party. The only external service contacted by the App is TikTok’s own API.

6. Retention and deletion

Historical metrics are kept for as long as the operator finds them useful, and can be deleted at any time by deleting the local files. Authorization can be revoked at any moment from TikTok’s own settings (Security and login → Manage app permissions); revoking it immediately ends the App’s access.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and processes no data from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version is published at this address with a new effective date.
